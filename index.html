Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding boards, admission procedures, batch timings, and demo interactions.

Which educational boards are covered at Physics World?
We cater comprehensively to students from CBSE, ICSE / ISC, and the West Bengal Board (WBCHSE / WBBSE). Study materials, problem sheets, and test evaluations are aligned specifically with each board's respective syllabus and question patterns.
Which classes and courses are offered?
We offer specialized batches for:
  • Class XI & XII Physics (Higher Secondary board preparation for CBSE, ISC, and WBCHSE)
  • NEET & JEE Physics (entrance focused problem solving and speed drills)
  • Class IX & X Physical Science (secondary science foundation for CBSE, ICSE, and WBBSE)
How can a student enroll or join an ongoing batch?
Enrolment is simple and direct. You can send an enquiry message on WhatsApp (+91 76991 81906) or call our numbers. We will discuss your current class, requirements, and assign the most suitable batch.
What are the batch timings and weekly class days?
Call or WhatsApp for current batch timings. Timings are scheduled across morning, afternoon, and evening batches depending on school hours and student groups at Pioneer Park near Barasat Station.
Is there a demo class or interaction session before joining?
Yes, prospective students and their parents are encouraged to attend an introductory interaction session with Tanmoy Bose. This allows students to experience our concept-first teaching methodology and discuss their individual academic roadmap. Please message on WhatsApp to reserve your slot.
